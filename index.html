01 / Cherry

it starts as a fruit.

A coffee bean is the seed of a cherry. It is picked when the skin has turned deep red and the fruit comes away with a light pull.

Where your coffee comes from, in one line.

Tell the sourcing story here: the regions and farms you buy from and how each lot is chosen.

02 / Pulping

the fruit comes off.

Squeezed, the skin splits and the bean slips out, still wet with a thin coat of sweet pulp.

03 / Drying

then it waits.

Spread out and turned for days, until it holds about 11% water. Dry, it keeps. Dry, it can travel.

04 / Roasting

heat does the rest.

In the drum the bean browns, swells and cracks open at around 196 °C. Everything you taste later is made in these few minutes.

05 / Grinding

broken open.

Ground moments before brewing, so the aroma ends up in the cup and not in the air.

06 / Brewing

and then, a cup.

Hot water, nine bars of pressure, under half a minute. The crema on top is the last thing the bean gives up.
